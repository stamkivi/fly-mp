A complete Drosophila connectome was given __N_BILLS__ real votes from the Estonian parliament and asked to take a position on each one.

Then the same brain was rewired at random — every neuron keeping its exact number of inputs and outputs — and asked again. __N_REWIRED__ times.

First, the chamber

Every one of the __N_MEMBERS__ members of the Riigikogu who belongs to a party, placed by nothing but how they voted. Bills in, positions out — no labels, no manifestos.

The space is recovered from the vote matrix alone. Faction colour is painted on afterwards, as a check, never as an input.

The axes are not ours

A voting space has no natural units, so this one is anchored to somebody else's: the Chapel Hill Expert Survey, where political scientists place each Estonian party on economic left–right and on the liberal–conservative axis.

Hold out an entire party and the map still puts its members within __ERR_Y__ on the vertical — but only __ERR_X__ on the horizontal. This parliament's roll-calls carry cultural position sharply and economic position barely. The picture has a sharp vertical and a soft horizontal, and that is a finding rather than a caveat.

Kärbes takes a seat

The fly reads each bill through its antennae — ten scored policy channels driving ten olfactory glomeruli, left side for against, right side for in favour — and the answer is read out of its descending neurons as a turn.

Over __N_BILLS__ bills that record lands it nearest __KARBES_PARTY__.

The input–output mapping is engineered, not discovered. There are no aye neurons in a fly.

Run the same brain again

Same connectome, same bills, different input noise — __N_REAL__ runs in all. If the seat were an artefact of the random spike trains, these would land anywhere.

They move __WITHIN__ compass units. Any difference smaller than that means nothing.

Now cut every wire and reconnect it at random

__N_REWIRED__ degree-preserving shuffles. Every neuron keeps its in-degree, its out-degree, its transmitter sign and its total output. The only thing that changes is who reaches whom.

What this is and is not

It is not a prediction. The real connectome scores __KARBES_AUC__ at telling which bills advanced, and some of the shuffled brains score higher — the best of them __AUC_BEST_FLY__. A logistic regression on the same scored channels plus the one procedural bit gets __AUC_REG__. That bit on its own gets __AUC_BIT__.

The regression is fitted to the outcome and the fly never sees it, so the comparison flatters the regression. It is still the right comparison: if prediction were the goal, nobody would simulate a brain to do it.

It is a statement about individuality. Identical parts, identical statistics, identical input, identical readout — and the wiring alone decides which politician you get.

How a bill becomes a smell

Each bill is scored on ten policy channels drawn from the topics Estonian voters actually rank — cost of living, healthcare, defence, social security, taxes, education, wages, energy, business, immigration. Each channel drives one named olfactory glomerulus. The sign of the score picks the side of the head, so a bill that raises costs and cuts defence smells asymmetric, and the brain turns.

The vote is the sign of that turn, read from the DNa family of descending neurons — the cells a real fly uses to steer — past a dead band measured on the network itself. Nothing is tuned to agree with any faction. The primary statistic is threshold-free.

The accuracy question, answered and closed

Cross-validated on the same __N_BILLS__ bills: content alone reaches __AUC_CONTENT__; who tabled the bill alone reaches __AUC_BIT__; both together reach __AUC_REG__. In this parliament almost every government bill advances and almost no member's bill does, so the procedural fact is nearly the whole signal and a linear model takes it immediately.

The fly gets __KARBES_AUC__ from the same information routed through 166,700 neurons. That is the pre-committed negative result, reported plainly: the connectome adds nothing over a small linear readout.

What the control is for

A degree-preserving shuffle keeps every summary statistic a connectome has: each neuron's number of partners, its transmitter, its total output. If the fly's politics survived the shuffle unchanged, the politics would live in those statistics and the connectome would be doing no work. If it lands somewhere else, the specific wiring is doing the work. That was written down before the shuffles were run.

Simulated. A leaky integrate-and-fire model of a measured connectome, not a fly. No claim is made about what the fly experiences, and the mapping from bills to odours to votes is engineered rather than discovered.
