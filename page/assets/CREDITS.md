# Assets

- `fly.png` — house-fly illustration from Orkin's blog (orkin.com, "10 common flies"), chosen by
  Sten on 2026-09-19. Not under a free licence: Orkin's copyright, used at the owner's own risk.
  It is a house fly, not a *Drosophila*. Matted off its white background by
  `scripts/cut_fly.py --white` (the source is kept under `data/raw/assets/`). The previous
  asset, André Karwath's dorsal *Drosophila melanogaster* (CC BY-SA 2.5), is reproducible from
  the same script without `--white`.
- `brain.jpg` / `brain.json` — rendered by `karbes/plate.py` from MaleCNS v1.0 `somaLocation`
  coordinates (CC BY 4.0). Not a photograph of a specimen. `brain.json` records the voxel
  frame it was drawn in so the soma atlas can be projected onto it.
- `hall.jpg` — the Riigikogu session hall from the Speaker's desk. Foto: Paul Kuimet,
  Riigikogu. Used unaltered, non-commercially, with attribution, under the Riigikogu photo
  archive terms (https://www.riigikogu.ee/riigikogu-fotoarhiivi-kasutustingimused/).
  Not to be modified or redistributed separately.
